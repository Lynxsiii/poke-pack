# Site overhaul brief

Scope: the public home, navigation, pack-selection and reveal journey, with collection and account surfaces sharing the same system. Mode: experience.

Audience: collectors arriving to open a real-set virtual booster; returning players managing a saved collection. The action is to choose a set, open a pack, understand the pull, and continue collecting. Proof is the real set art, card scans, odds, and visible progress. Accounts and saved data remain intact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A card print proof laid out for the player to handle. The site leads with the pack and card instead of a promotional preamble or a grid of equal product tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Saturated press blue holds the opening zone; cool paper and deep ink organize set data and collection tools. A single warm red marks the action and pull state. Trim rules, edition tabs, and small registration marks provide rhythm without competing with card artwork. Controls read like physical labels on the proof, not generic glowing pills.

STORY: Pick a current set immediately, see its real wrapper and cost, open it, flip through cards, then move naturally into the binder or another pack. Odds and values remain legible throughout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact header with Packs as the leading destination. The home screen places a large featured wrapper and visible set choice opposite a short promise and one primary action. On mobile, the pack and action remain above the long-form catalog and community content.

FORM: Print-run proof sheet at the press table, the third grounded candidate. Seed a6253f78. The signature moment is the wrapper giving way to a focused card reveal, with the final spread acting as a printed pull sheet.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
