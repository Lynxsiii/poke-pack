# Cosmic Eclipse (sm12)

Built 2026-09-30. Research: `/mnt/project-files/sun-moon/research/sm12.md`.

- Cards: pkmn.gg `cosmic-eclipse`, 272 entries: #1-271 (236 printed, 27 full arts #210-236, secrets #237-271) plus `143a`, the Togepi & Cleffa & Igglybuff-GX full art with the Yellow A stamp that only the Small But Mighty Premium Collection holds (TCGplayer 226128, 10 booster packs, Bulbapedia). `143a` is filed as a promo of the set (`promoCards["143a"]`, `like: "143a"`, `keepScan`) so it stays out of the pack pools. scrydex `sm12-272` is the card back; there is no #272. 271 cards in packs plus 13 promos = 284 (`totalCount`).
- Packs: 11 cards, `sm19` Energy, no reverse Energy. The twelve Character Rares (#237-248, pkmn.gg `Rare Secret` with `supertype: "Pokémon"`) sit in the reverse slot (sturzflugbombardieru, Elite Fourum /157; ThePriceDex's reverse table sums to 1.007 only with them there). They carry `character: true` in data/sm12.json. The nine gold Trainers/Energy #263-271 are the rare slot's `secret`.
- ENGINE: `js/engine.js` needs a `character` pool and `secret` without them (patch in the builder report). Until then a pack can roll an undefined pool.
- Odds (data/pullrates.json): sturzflugbombardieru 3,024 packs pooled with burpies' 3,136 (Elite Fourum). Holo 14.31% is sturzflug only. Rainbow 1.50% and gold 0.89% come from burpies only (sturzflug's table has the two combined). ThePriceDex restates sturzflug's model and is not independent.
- Foil maps: TCG Live `sm12`, numbers as printed, checked by overlay. 112 cards carry a map: 22 Rare Holo, 16 GX, 27 full arts, 14 rainbows, 21 Rare Secret (the 12 characters and 9 golds), plus 12 promos. 50 have an etch map (the 27 full arts, 14 rainbows and the nine golds; the twelve Character Rares have none) and so do smp229/230. No map: `143a`, plain Rares, commons, uncommons.
- Products (TCGplayer): Elite Trainer Box (8 packs, `elite-trainer-box`), Build & Battle Box (one of smp218-221), Celebi 3-pack blister (smp224), Mismagius and Terrakion single blisters (smp222, smp223), TAG TEAM Generations Premium Collection (smp229, smp230; 7 packs), Fall 2019 Collector's Chest (smp226-228; 5 packs), Small But Mighty Premium Collection (143a; 10 packs). The last three are filed in TCGplayer's Miscellaneous group (`tcgplayerGroup: 2374`) and hold packs of unnamed sets; the packs here are Cosmic Eclipse's, which the product note says. Left out, no TCGplayer market price: Victini 3-pack blister (smp225), Pikachu-GX & Eevee-GX Special Collection (smp232-235).
