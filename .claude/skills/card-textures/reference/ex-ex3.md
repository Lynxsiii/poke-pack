# EX era: EX Dragon (ex3)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder e1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/ex/research/ex3.md`, `ex3-products.md`, `ex-pack-structure.md`, `ex-finish-types.md`, `second-source-check.md`. Release 2003-11-24 (Serebii and TCGplayer agree). 100 cards (97 printed, 98-100 are the secrets).

## Cards
Rare Holo 12 (1-12: Absol, Altaria, Crawdaunt, Flygon, Golem, Grumpig, Minun, Plusle, Roselia, Salamence, Shedinja, Torkoal), Rare Holo EX 9 (89-97: Ampharos ex, Dragonite ex, Golem ex, Kingdra ex, Latias ex, Latios ex, Magcargo ex, Muk ex, Rayquaza ex), Rare Secret 3 (98 Charmander, 99 Charmeleon, 100 Charizard; in PACKS, not box toppers: the first secret rares of the EX era), Rare 10, Uncommon 33, Common 33. No Energy. Counts match pkmn.gg. Scans: scrydex `ex3-<n>`; looked at 1, 10, 50, 80, 89, 97, 99, 100 (the secrets and an ex included): straight, none swapped; the automatic edge check flagged none.

## Pack
Nine cards, early layout: the holo sheet (12 holos + the 3 secrets) and the 9 ex come in the fifth slot. `exSlots({early: true, holo: .1349, ex: .1667, secret: .0318})`: EF post 37's holo sheet of 36/216 split as 12 holos at 0.1349 and the 3 secrets at an assumed H7 share each (0.0318 together; a 3-box tally saw 1.33 secrets a box, PokeGym 2667 says as rare as other holos): medium-low on the secrets. The second print run (reverse TV Reporter on the sheet, 88 reverse cards) is dealt. `reverseTwice` empty (the 22 doubled reverse commons are not named).

## For the finish agents
Cosmos holo on holos, ex and the three secret Charmander line cards (`foil-ex ex-ex ex-secret` only for ex-named; the three secrets are plain-named Pokemon so `foil-holo ex-cosmos ex-secret`). The dot strip is present on holo, ex, secret and reverse cards. Mirror reverse on 88 cards (no reverse for ex or secrets). No refractor, cracked ice, delta, topper.
