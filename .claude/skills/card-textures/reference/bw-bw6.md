# Black & White: Dragons Exalted (bw6)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c2). Research: `bw6.md`, `bw6-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Released 2012-08-15. 128 cards (124 printed + shiny Serperior, Reuniclus, Krookodile and Rayquaza). Rarities C39 U36 R24 RH13 EX6 RU6 RS4. First Dragon-type cards.

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy. Same sheet model; the biggest tally of the era (43 boxes, secrets observed 0.26 a box against the model's 0.33) and PokeBeach's remark that single boxes vary widely: holo .2407, EX .0556, full art .0278, secret .0093.
Pack $355.40 (TCGplayer market, product 98541). Rayquaza 128 (shiny secret) has no market price yet; the other 127 singles are priced. Prices contain two Blend Energy rows that needed a name rule in `norm()` (scripts/fetch_tcgplayer_prices.py: "Blend Energy GFPD" is "Blend Energy GrassFirePsychicDarkness").

## Scans
Scrydex. Looked at 1, 22, 24, 85, 123, 128: clean, no replacement.

## Maps
TCG Live key `bw6`: 29 of 29 foil prints (13 Rare Holo, 6 EX, 6 full art, 4 secret), 10 etch maps. Overlaid on Ho-Oh-EX 22 (the map follows the art out of the window), Rayquaza-EX 85 and 123, shiny Rayquaza 128: aligned. Promo maps for BW45-47, BW49, BW52.

## Wrappers
pokesymbols `dragons-exalted-pack-0..3`: Terrakion, Giratina, Rayquaza, Gyarados (Bulbapedia's table agrees).

## Products
Built: Legendary EX Tins [Mewtwo-EX] BW45, [Darkrai-EX] BW46, [Rayquaza-EX] BW47 (4 packs, typically 2 bw6 + 1 bw2 + 1 bw1), 3 Pack Blisters [Chandelure] and [Scolipede], Stage 1 blisters [Excadrill line] and [Lilligant line] (BW49), Double Album Box (5 packs; TCGplayer 206350 in Miscellaneous, no market price), Emerging Challenges Box (2 packs, BW52 Lillipup), the two Black & White Variety Blisters (3 packs). TCGplayer has no price for any of them, so all are priced from the packs ($355.40 each) plus promos. Every pack count is Bulbapedia only (medium-low). Reprints, theme decks, figures and the Dragons Exalted Prerelease promo BW48 Altaria (no product) are not simulated.
Promo foilAs: BW45-47 Rare Holo EX, BW49 and BW52 Rare Holo (map on each).
