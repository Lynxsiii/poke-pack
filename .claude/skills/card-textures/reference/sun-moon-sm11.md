# Unified Minds (sm11), built 2026-09-30

English, 2 August 2019. 236 printed, 258 cards in packs (21 Rare Holo, 17 GX, 23 full art, 13 rainbow, 9 gold), plus 12 promo cards (totalCount 270). Research: /mnt/project-files/sun-moon/research/sm11.md.

## Data
- Cards: pkmn.gg `sun-moon/unified-minds`, scans scrydex `sm11-<n>`. pkmn.gg lists 261; the three extras are League Cup / Regional prize reprints (79a Jirachi-GX, 191a Cherish Ball, 206a Reset Stamp), left out with `skipNumbers`.
- Wrappers: `unified-minds-pack-0..3` (Garchomp & Giratina, Umbreon & Darkrai, Espeon & Deoxys, Mewtwo & Mew), shipped as `pack-N-v2`.
- Foil maps: TCG Live key `sm11`, no swaps. 83 of 83 holo prints have a map, etch maps on the 45 full art, rainbow and gold cards. Promo maps from `smbsp`: all 12.
- Prices: tcgcsv group 2464, 258 of 258 singles.
