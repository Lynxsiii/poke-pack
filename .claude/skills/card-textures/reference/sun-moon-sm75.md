# Dragon Majesty (sm75)

Built 2026-09-30 (builder b3). 70 printed, 88 cards in `data/sm75.json`: 78 pack cards, the 40a print and 9 box promos. Research:
`/mnt/project-files/sun-moon/research/sm75.md`.

## Sources
- Cards pkmn.gg `sun-moon/dragon-majesty`, scans scrydex `sm75-<n>`. Pack: 1 Energy
  (`sm17`), 5 C, 3 U, 1 reverse, 1 rare slot; no plain Rare exists, so the rare slot is a
  Rare Holo or better (Flipside Gaming Collector's Dossier: "at least one holographic
  rare card and there are no non-holo rares",
  https://flipsidegaming.com/blogs/pokemon-blog/sun-and-moon-dragon-majesty-collectors-dossier).
  Holo = 1 minus the hits (0.7456), structural.
- Odds: Elite Fourum (n=389, one source and a small sample, flagged by Elite Fourum
  itself for special sets): GX 15.42%, Prism Star 10.8% (reverse slot), full art 6.94%,
  rainbow 2.31%, gold 0.77% (3 pulls; the four golds share it). ThePriceDex rounds the
  same data and disagrees on rainbow and gold, so it was not used.
- No Booster Box was printed (Generations-style special set): packs sold loose at about
  $5; the booster pack's TCGplayer market price today is $107.42, the cost on the site.

## Maps
TCG Live key `sm7-5`. All 32 holo-class set cards have a foil map, 14 an etch map.
Promos smp125, 126, 136, 139, 140, 141, 142, 155, 156 have `smbsp` maps, etch on 139,
141, 155, 156. 40a borrows card 40's window map.

## Oddities
- The 40 and 40a Altaria are different arts. 40a (Shin Nagasawa) is the special-art
  reprint in Lost Thunder's Altaria blister (sm8's `like: "sm75:40a"`): `promo: true`,
  sold as a single (`altaria-40a`). 60a Fiery Flint (Pokemon League) is skipped.
- smp155 Kingdra-GX and smp156 Dragonite-GX are rainbow full arts (`foilAs` Rare
  Rainbow), the other GX promos regular art (`Rare Holo GX`); the research calls some of
  them "full art", the scans say otherwise.
- Products built: ETB (10 packs, smp125 Naganadel-GX), Latios Pin Collection (smp136),
  Super-Premium Collection (10 packs, smp155 and smp156), Ultra Necrozma GX Figure
  Collection (4 packs, one source), Salamence GX and White Kyurem GX Special Collections
  (5 packs each, two promos). Left out, no TCGplayer market price today: Latias Pin
  Collection (smp135) and Legends of Unova GX Premium Collection (6 packs, smp137,
  smp138).
