# XY: Primal Clash (xy5)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder b2). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/xy/research/xy5.md`, `xy5-products.md`, `xyp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Release 2015-02-04. 164 cards (160 printed + 4 gold secrets: Dive Ball, Enhanced Hammer, Switch, Weakness Policy) plus the 4 promos below (counted under xyp).

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy (same sources as xy4). E2 sheet counts against E2's 28 boxes and a 30-box PokeBeach tally: holo .1556, EX .0926, Mega EX .0185 (low), full art .0587, secret .0080 (low, on the full-art sheet). Medium confidence. data/pullrates.json.
pkmn.gg's "Nidoran ♀" is "Nidoran (F)" at TCGplayer: `norm()` in scripts/fetch_tcgplayer_prices.py now drops the gender signs.

## Maps
TCG Live key `xy5`: 50 of 50 foil prints (18 Rare Holo, 12 Rare Holo EX, 16 Rare Ultra, 4 Rare Secret), 20 etch maps. Overlaid on scans (Aegislash, Trevenant-EX, Primal Kyogre-EX FA, Weakness Policy): fine.

## Products
All 11 confirmed products are built: both Elite Trainer Boxes (8 packs), the Ditto three-pack blister (XY40), Legend of Hoenn Tins [Kyogre EX] (XY41) and [Groudon EX] (XY42), the Primal Kyogre (XY51) and Primal Groudon (XY52) Collections, Flygon EX Box (XY61), and the Furfrou, Rhyperior and Zoroark blisters (their Cosmos Holofoil reprint cards belong to another set and are not simulated). The collections, tins and box hold 2 xy5 + 1 xy4 + 1 xy3 packs (one retail extract each, medium; `from` in the config).
TCGplayer prices the Ditto blister, both tins and the Flygon box; the others carry `priceDerived: "packs"` (packs x the $64.38 pack, plus promos). Prerelease XY39 Kingdra has no product. Booster box $3,799.99 (TCGplayer, a thin market).
