# EX era: EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua (ex4)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder e1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/ex/research/ex4.md`, `ex4-products.md`, `box-contents.md`, `ex-pack-structure.md`, `ex-finish-types.md`, `second-source-check.md`. Release 2004-03-15 (Serebii and TCGplayer agree). 97 cards (95 printed). The catalog name is "Team Magma vs Team Aqua" (the printed logo): "EX Team Magma vs Team Aqua Master" is over test-achievements' 32-character title limit; the data and product names keep "EX".

## Cards
Rare Holo 12 (1-12, six Team Aqua and six Team Magma Pokemon), Rare Holo EX 7 (89-95: Blaziken ex, Cradily ex, Entei ex, Raikou ex, Sceptile ex, Suicune ex, Swampert ex), Rare Secret 2 (96 Absol, the BOX TOPPER, `cardFlags {"96": {"boxTopper": true}}`, never dealt; 97 Jirachi, in packs), Rare 12 (88 Double Rainbow Energy), Uncommon 32 (86 Aqua Energy, 87 Magma Energy), Common 32. No basic Energy. Counts match pkmn.gg. Scans: scrydex `ex4-<n>`; looked at 1, 9, 60, 75, 88, 89, 96, 97: straight, none swapped; the automatic edge check flagged none.

## Pack
Nine cards, the first LATE layout: 5 Commons, 2 Uncommons, a Mirror reverse (88 cards), a rare slot that is a holo 0.2318, ex 0.0833, Jirachi 0.0182 or else a plain Rare (0.6667). `exSlots({holo: .2318, ex: .0833, secret: .0182})`. Elite Fourum post 38 (24 non-holo rares, 9 holos, 3 ex a box) with Potomac's "1 foil card, 1 rare card, 5 common, 2 uncommon" and PokeGym 19395; Jirachi is a 3-box tally (medium-low). `reverseTwice: ["88"]`: post 38's text names Double Rainbow Energy as the one reverse rare printed twice; the 21 doubled reverse uncommons are only in the post's table image, so they are not listed.

## For the finish agents
Cosmos holo on the holos, the ex and the two secrets (Absol and Jirachi are plain-named: `foil-holo ex-cosmos ex-secret`). The dot strip: absent on holos, commons, uncommons and Trainers; present on the Pokemon-ex, Absol and Jirachi scans (COL says the ultra/secret cards kept it: the scans agree, so no foil over it). Mirror reverse is the LAST of its kind (body foiled, art box plain). Absol is never in a pack: it exists in the data for the binder/dex only.
