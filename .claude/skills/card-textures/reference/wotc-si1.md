# Southern Islands (si1)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder w4). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/wotc/research/si1.md`, `si1-products.md`; `SCAFFOLD-NOTES.md`.
Release 2001-07-31. 18 cards, all rarity "Promo" (`rarityOverrides` 1-18); the set is a `promoSet` + `kitSet` like xy0: `packSize 0`, no slots, no wrappers, one collection product.

## Cards and scans
Scrydex `si1-<n>`, two sizes, numerically sorted. Cards 1, 4, 7, 11, 14 and 17 are the reverse holofoil prints and their scans carry the foil baked in; the lead's decision is that they stay plain (no extra holo finish). Each promoCards entry is `{"like":"si1:N","keepScan":true}`. Logo and symbol mirrored to `assets/cards/sets/si1-logo.webp` and `si1-symbol.webp`.

## Product
`southern-islands-collection` (TCGplayer 610709): all 18 cards, always the same; the real folder also held a card list, six postcards and three booster packs of an unrecorded set, so no pack is simulated. Priced at TCGplayer's listing, $17,925, which is a $17,000 low and no sales (the folder sells sealed). `sealed.json` carries an empty `si1` entry.
