# wotc-shining working notes

## Research findings (2026-10-01; refs/SOURCES.md)

### Neo Destiny (neo4 106-113): the figure is the foil
- WHERE: the Pokemon figure only (plus Charizard's flame blast under the tail: it sparkles red and lights orange in three photos).
  Background scene, the white glow outline, frame, text: plain print. Confidence high (10+ photos over 8 cards).
- PATTERN: no stars, no cosmos. A metallic foil with a FINE SPARKLE GRAIN (Charizard eBay zoom: 2-4 px specks on a 950 px
  card = about 1/300 of the card width; Kabutops tumblr, Tyranitar SCI, Steelix Ricardo all grainy). The rv-j3b "smooth mirror,
  no glitter" call was wrong about the grain; it was right that the light is a continuous sheen (the grain rides on it).
- COLOUR: three states in the photos:
  1. away from the lamp: dark metal, like the scans (navy-violet Charizard, navy Mewtwo, black-olive others);
  2. lamp on the figure: a bright SILVER tinted by the printed ink: Charizard lavender/pink-violet, Mewtwo silver-mauve/lavender,
     Celebi peach-rose (lower body maroon), Kabutops silver-beige, Raichu gold-yellow, Steelix gold-beige, Tyranitar pale gold,
     Noctowl gold-olive;
  3. at the diffraction angle: a SPECTRAL BAND across the figure, either direction (nerdventure: green-yellow-red-magenta-blue
     left to right; Yahoo: red-yellow-green-cyan-blue-violet), one spectrum spanning most of the figure; Mewtwo shows it on
     part of the figure only (fist and tail) with mauve silver elsewhere: a band that slides, not a fill.
- The scan's hue is NOT the lit tint for the warm cards (Steelix, Tyranitar, Noctowl, Raichu scan cyan/olive-blue, light gold):
  tints come from photos, per card.

### Neo Revelation (neo3 65, 66): the background is the foil
- WHERE: the art window minus the Pokemon (and minus the kayaker on Gyarados); thin background ink (Magikarp's faint background
  fish) still shows glints. Confidence high (6 photos + Bleeding Cool).
- PATTERN: the standard Neo galaxy foil: dots and discs of many sizes in loose clusters (our scans show them frozen, white-blue).
- COLOUR: flat or away: the dark printed sky with multicoloured glints (green, cyan, pink, red); near the specular angle the whole
  background goes a silver-lavender mirror haze with the drawing through it; the discs flash blue/cyan.
