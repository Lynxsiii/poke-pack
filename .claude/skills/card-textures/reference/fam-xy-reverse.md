Module js/fam/xy-reverse.js, sheet css/holo/fam/xy-reverse.css, maps assets/foil/fam/xy-reverse/rev/<set>-<n>.webp, scripts

**Real card** (Collexy photos of Illumise xy1-9, Rhyperior xy5-77, Braixen xy10-12, Blacksmith xy6-88; TCG Live ph maps; the earlier note
reference/xy-holo-reverse.md): the body but the art window and the yellow border is one holographic mirror; the type's big ghosted symbols
(Poke Balls on a Trainer) show brighter than the field; the text is opaque ink over it. Rainbow columns slide with the tilt.

**Built**: `xyrev-map` = xy-generations' `xygen-rev` recipe (TCG Live ph map as mask with the ink knocked out at generation time, vertical rainbow
columns colour-dodged, rest opacity 0.62, glare soft-light) on every reverse the CDN maps. Faces without a map (xy0, kits, promos, Energy) keep the
era's tile drawing (css/holo/xy.css).

**Judge**: phone size WebKit, rest / 30:25 / 75:65 / 50:78; the text must stay black (compare bare vs foil ink at 50:78), the art window matte, dark
bodies (Yveltal) a saturated rainbow with crescents, pale bodies (Pikachu, Sycamore) a soft rainbow with symbols.

**Open**: yellow-on-yellow (Pikachu) and white Trainer bodies show the colour-dodge only faintly; no Chromium sheets; 29.75 MB of maps.
