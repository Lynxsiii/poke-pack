# ex-pokemon-ex: research notes

Family: every EX-era Pokémon-ex print that is not a refractor (136 faces: Rare Holo EX of ex1-ex14 and ex16, the secret
ex, the np/ex promo ex, the cracked-ice ex of ex5 and ex6). Sources: refs/SOURCES.md.

## Finish 1: Pokémon-ex cosmos (130 faces)

Seen on: Mewtwo ex1-101, Electabuzz ex1-97, Gardevoir ex2-96, Aggron ex2-95, Latios/Latias ex3-94/93, Rayquaza ex3-97,
Golem/Kingdra/Muk ex3, Ninetales ex5-96, Charizard ex6-105 (two graded copies), Clefable/Gengar/Gyarados ex6,
Rocket's Mewtwo ex7-99, Sharpedo ex8-104, Blaziken/Swampert ex14, Kyogre ex promos (YouTube frames from 20+ videos,
Elite Fourum close-ups, the cosmos copies of the FRLG birds on Collexy).

- Where the foil is: the OUTER SILVER BORDER (card edge to the coloured body, all four sides, including the wide
  e-Reader foot of ex1-ex4 with its logo and dot strip printed on it: the scans show cosmos dots through the logo
  and between the strip lines) and the ART WINDOW (inside its grey frame line, the Pokémon included: ink printed
  over the foil). Matte: the name bar, the HP/type, the coloured body (including its darker edge band), the grey
  window frame line, the evolution ring (stage cards), the BASIC/STAGE bar, the ex-rule strip, the text box, the
  weakness row and copyright line. The yellow border of the np001/np002 promos is plain ink: only the window is foil.
- Pattern and scale: a mirror-silver ground carrying "cosmos" pinpoints: round and clover-shaped dots of many sizes
  (1-8 px on a 600-px scan, a few larger orbs/swirls), dense on the border, through the art. The finest points are
  below scan resolution. The scan already shows the dots it caught (frozen colours).
- Colour behaviour: each dot glints in one saturated colour (cyan, green, yellow, orange, magenta, violet, white)
  that changes as the card tips; the ground flashes silver-white near the mirror angle, with a rainbow cast on the
  light parts of the art (Gardevoir's dress: pink, green, cyan); dark ink stays dark with tinted dots in it.
- Motion: tipping sweeps a broad bright band across the foil; the dots twinkle on and off individually (small
  gratings, narrow angular response), more of them lit at grazing angles; away from the light the border goes
  mid-grey with only scattered points.
- Confidence: high on the zones (consistent across sets, videos and scans); medium on the exact dot density.

## Finish 2: cracked ice ex (6 faces: ex5-97/98/99 Regice/Regirock/Registeel ex, ex6-114/115/116 Articuno/Moltres/Zapdos ex)

Seen on: Regice, Regirock, Registeel ex (Collexy studio photos, PokePrice and other videos), the FRLG secret birds
(Collexy cracked + cosmos copies, Elite Fourum PSA slabs and an angled Moltres).

- Where: the same zones as the cosmos ex (silver border + art window, the Pokémon included); the name bar, body and
  text box are flat. (Some copies lost the layer and look like plain cosmos: the site's scans are cracked ice.)
- Pattern and scale: a sheet of flat angular shards (triangles and quadrilaterals) of very different sizes, roughly
  1.5-6 per cent of the card width, no outlines, random orientations.
- Colour behaviour: each shard is one flat colour at a time (pastel on the bare border: cyan, pink, yellow,
  lavender, mint; saturated and darker in the window under the ink); most are close to the print, a few flash
  bright silver-white or a strong colour, and which ones changes as the card tips.
- Motion: shards switch on and off individually as the angle passes their facet orientation; no smooth sweep inside a
  shard. The Zapdos bird itself shows few shards (opaque yellow ink).
- Confidence: high on zones and the look; the scan's frozen facets give the per-card shard layout.

## Measurements from the scans (see the generator)

Per card: the coloured body rectangle (inner edge of the silver border) by the strongest colour step inside a per-set
range (verified on edge sheets for all 136 faces); the art window from a per-set template (aggregated edge profiles of
every card in the set, offsets from the body edges: frame-to-art step); the evolution ring from a per-set fit on the
mean of the stage cards (outer ellipse ~50x41 px, centre ~19 px right and ~20 px below the window's bottom-left corner);
body corner radii per frame (ex1-ex4 rounded name bar ~20 px, others 4-8 px).
