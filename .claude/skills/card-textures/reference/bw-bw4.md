# Black & White: Next Destinies (bw4)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c2). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/bw/research/bw4.md`, `bw4-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`, `bw-pack-structure.md`.
Released 2012-02-08. 103 cards (99 printed + 4 shiny secrets: Emboar, Chandelure, Zoroark, Hydreigon). pkmn.gg and pokemontcg.io both say 103; rarities C29 U27 R21 RH10 EX6 RU6 RS4. The first Pokemon-EX (regular art, `Rare Holo EX`) and the first textured full arts.

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy: 5 Commons, 3 Uncommons, a reverse holo, a rare slot (Elite Fourum per-set table #104, ToyWiz "[10 Cards]", Bulbapedia Booster pack page, wrapper text). Rare-slot rates are Elite Fourum's 216-pack case model (holo 52, EX 12, full art 6, secret 2) against its 28 tallied boxes, PokeBeach box reports and Flipside's ratios: holo .2407, EX .0556, full art .0278, secret .0093. EX and full art high confidence (three sources), secret medium (EF and PokeBeach 1 box in 3, Flipside 1 in 2). data/pullrates.json carries the links.
Pack price $357.29 is TCGplayer's market price (group 1412, product 98538); no booster box market exists.

## Scans
Scrydex. Looked at 1, 5 (Shaymin-EX), 20 (Chandelure), 94 (full art Shaymin-EX), 100 (Emboar), 103 (Hydreigon): clean, no white edges. No scan replaced. TCGplayer prices matched 103/103 singles.

## Maps
TCG Live key `bw4`: 26 of 26 foil prints have a map (10 Rare Holo, 6 EX, 6 full art, 4 secret), 10 etch maps (full arts only; the shiny secrets have none). Overlaid on the scans (Chandelure 20, Shaymin-EX 5 and 94, Emboar 100): the window holo covers the art window only, the EX map takes art and border, the full art the whole card, the secret the Pokemon and the type symbol. Promo maps from `bwbsp` for BW34, BW36-39, BW41-43 (BW33 Riolu has none on TCG Live).

## Wrappers
pokesymbols `next-destinies-pack-0..3` (opaque, cut with clean_pack_art.py in place): Zekrom, Reshiram, Regigigas, Mewtwo. The art was looked at on a dark background; the names match Bulbapedia's wrapper table.
