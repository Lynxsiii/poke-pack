# Black & White: Dark Explorers (bw5)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c2). Research: `bw5.md`, `bw5-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Released 2012-05-09. 111 cards (108 printed + shiny Gardevoir 109, shiny Archeops 110 and the first secret Item, Pokemon Catcher 111). Rarities C33 U35 R19 RH9 EX6 RU6 RS3.

## Pack
Same ten-card shape and sheet model as bw4 (Elite Fourum #105: "Dark Explorers has the same box ratios as Next Destinies", 28 tallied boxes, EX 2.00 and full art 1.07 per box observed), with PokeBeach's sealed case (12 regular EX and 6 full arts in 6 boxes, a third source) and Flipside: holo .2407, EX .0556, full art .0278, secret .0093. Confidence as bw4 (EX and full art high, secret medium).
TCGplayer has no market price for the pack (product 98521: lowest listing $394.00, median listing $396.63, no sales): `data/sealed.json` and the set cost use the lowest listing, $394.

## Scans
Scrydex, 111 of 111 singles priced. Looked at 1, 3, 63, 67 (Scraggy), 68 (Scrafty), 107, 110, 111: clean. No scan replaced.

## Maps
TCG Live key `bw5`: 24 of 24 foil prints (9 Rare Holo, 6 EX, 6 full art, 3 secret), 9 etch maps. Overlaid (Venusaur, Darkrai-EX 63 and 107, Archeops): aligned. Promo maps for BW35 and BW44.

## Wrappers
pokesymbols `dark-explorers-pack-0..3`: Scrafty, Excadrill, Darkrai, Venusaur. Bulbapedia's wrapper table lists Kyurem for the fourth wrapper; the image shows Venusaur (the research looked at it too), so the image wins. Pack 0 is Scrafty, not Scraggy (compare cards 67 and 68).

## Products
Built: 3 Pack Blisters [Luxray] (TCGplayer 477179) and [Krookodile] (477180) (3 packs, text "3 Dark Explorers booster packs, 1 coin, and a promotional card"), Stage 2 blisters [Luxray line] and [Krookodile line] (2 packs), Stage 1 blisters [Persian line] (BW35 Meowth) and [Lucario line] (1 pack), Kyurem Box (4 packs, BW44 Kyurem), Superb Serperior, Excellent Emboar and Spectacular Samurott Boxes (2 packs each). Pack counts beyond the two 3 Pack Blisters are Bulbapedia only; the boxes' theme decks, figures, Sampling Packs and the Cracked Ice and Cosmos reprints are not simulated. BW40 Volcarona (Prerelease) has no product; BW33 Riolu, also in the Lucario-line blister, is filed under bw4. Raiders and Shadows theme decks and the Sampling Pack are left to the deck builder.
Promo foilAs: BW35 and BW44 Rare Holo (TCG Live has maps for both).
