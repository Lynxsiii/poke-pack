# Diamond & Pearl: Secret Wonders (dp3)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder d1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/dp/research/dp3.md`, `dp3-products.md`, `dpp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Release 2007-11-07. 132 cards (C 45, U 41, R 24, RH 20, LV.X 2: Gardevoir 131, Honchkrow 132), plus two promos filed as `dp3-dppNN`.

## Pack
Ten cards, no basic Energy. Rare .6667, Rare Holo .3194, LV.X .0139 (half a box: Elite Fourum's sample was 4 LV.X in 8 boxes and 2 in 3 boxes also fits; Flipside's "1:36 early" is for the first sets, so this one has no clean second source, medium-low). data/pullrates.json.

## Price
tcgcsv (the daily feed) has no price for the booster pack (98569); TCGplayer's own price endpoint (mpapi.tcgplayer.com/v2/product/98569/pricepoints) says market $568.75, which is what data/sealed.json carries. Thin, like the other DP packs.

## Scans and wrappers
Scrydex `dp3-<n>`; looked at Ampharos (first), Lugia, Mew, Charizard, Gardevoir LV.X, Honchkrow LV.X (last), Weavile, Roserade: clean. Wrappers `secret-wonders-pack-0..3` cut in place: Honchkrow, Roserade, Gallade, Weavile.

## Maps
None.

## Products
Two built: Diamond & Pearl Value Pack 2 [Buizel] DP13 and [Chatot] DP14 (four Diamond & Pearl-series packs and an Energy coin in the real pack; the mix of the three sets is not recorded, so all four open as Secret Wonders; the Gible and Pikachu reprints are not simulated). Derived price (4 x the pack plus the promo).
Left out: booster box (no price), theme decks Lavaflow and Powerhouse (no TCGplayer listing, one source), Shinx DP15 and Pikachu DP16 products (Value Pack 1 / Trainer's Challenge Set / Deck Pack conflict, low), Collector's Poster Box (one source), prerelease Kirlia stamp (T only). The Lucario tin (4 packs, 3 of them Secret Wonders) is filed under dp2.
