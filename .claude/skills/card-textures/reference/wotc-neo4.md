# Wizards of the Coast: Neo Destiny (neo4)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder w3). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/wotc/research/neo4.md`, `neo4-products.md`, `wotc-pack-structure.md`, `theme-decks.md`.
Release 2002-02-28. English Unlimited prints only. 113 cards (printed 105): Rare Holo 16, Rare 19, Uncommon 37, Common 33, **Rare Shining 8**. Light and Dark Pokémon are regular holos or rares. No Energy.

## Scans
All 113 checked: aligned, no borders, 1st Edition stamp throughout; none swapped. pkmn.gg names the Dark Exeggutor card "Dark Exeggcutor"; the price script's norm() corrects it.

## Pack
11 cards: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons, a rare slot (Rare, Rare Holo or Shining from its own sheet); no reverse. `wotcSlots({ holo: 0.25, rareSpecial: [["shining", 0.0833]] })`. Elite Fourum's 216-pack sequence: 54 holos, 144 Rares, 18 Shinings (0.25, 0.667, 0.0833); 14 whole boxes average 3.00 Shinings, the same 0.0833. PSA "one to three a box", Flipside "about 1:12": high. Not modelled: the late "error packs" where a Shining takes a Common slot.

## Wrapper
Four, `neo-destiny-pack-N`: Tyranitar, Noctowl, Togetic, Celebi (neo4-11, 110, 15, 106).

## Prices
All 113 priced: Shining Charizard 107 $1,700.99, Dark Gengar 6 $1,101.50, Dark Espeon 4 $649.99. **Pack $1,610** (mid of live listings, no market price). Booster box 563755 derived from packs; theme deck Light (232869, shop price $299.96, its TCGplayer mid) shop-visible through its promo neo4-13.
