# ex-reverse: working notes (family agent, 2026-10-01)

Scope: every EX-era reverse holo face (ex1-ex16, np reverse promos 3/8/10). 1,493 reverse-eligible cards in ex1-ex16
(Common/Uncommon/Rare/Rare Holo + ex1's six basic Energy); census counts 1,885 faces (every EX card x reverse).

## What the real cards do (photos: refs/SOURCES.md)
- ex1-ex4 Mirror: the WHOLE card but the art picture is a bright prismatic rainbow foil (border, name bar, text box,
  footer, dot strip included); the art box is matte. Photos (E4 poll post, eBay) show saturated broad spectral bands.
- ex5-ex16: the card body is matte; the foil is in the ART BOX BACKGROUND with the set's pattern; the Pokémon (the main
  subject) is matte (white underlay). Delta exception (E4 zomus table): the foil is ON the Pokémon instead of the background
  for ex11/ex13 Holo Rare delta and for every delta card of ex14/ex15.
  - ex5 Energy symbols, big, equal size, columns of the same symbol; Rare/Holo Rare add a foil type symbol in the attack box.
  - ex6 Energy symbols of various sizes + an underlying cosmos sparkle; Rare/Holo Rare add a foil Poké Ball in the attack box.
  - ex7 small symbols + cosmos sparkle; set stamp (holo on commons, coloured on rares); rares gold name/HP.
  - ex8 Deoxys "wheel/pinwheel": a fine tessellation of triangular facets, strongly holographic.
  - ex9 front-facing Poké Balls, bubbles, fireworks starbursts.
  - ex10 3D Poké Balls in several perspectives, tiny pluses.
  - ex11, ex13-ex16 "plain": a smooth vivid RAINBOW foil on the background art (no pattern).
  - ex12 cosmos sparkle.
