# Hidden Fates and the Shiny Vault (sm115)

Built 2026-09-30. Research: `/mnt/project-files/sun-moon/research/sm115.md` (sources below).

- Cards: pkmn.gg `hidden-fates` (69: 68 printed plus the rainbow Moltres & Zapdos & Articuno-GX) and `shiny-vault` (94, `gallery: "sv"`, numbers `SV1`-`SV94`, scrydex `sma-SV1`, TCGplayer group 2594). Card ids are `sm115-SV49`. 163 cards, plus 8 promos filed under the set = 171 (`totalCount`).
- Shiny Vault tiers on pkmn.gg: Rare Shiny SV1-45, Rare Shiny GX SV46-80, Rare Ultra (full-art Trainers) SV81-86, Rare Secret (gold stadiums SV87-90, Tapu GX SV91-94). The engine pools `shinyBaby`, `shinyGX`, `shinyUltra`, `shinyGold` read exactly these.
- Packs: 11 cards (Energy 1, Common 5, Uncommon 3, reverse slot 1, rare slot 1), `sm19` Energy. Hidden Fates packs were never sold on their own (pokemon.com via pocketmonsters.net/news/3686; Bulbapedia): the shop's `boosterPack` price is the loose-pack TCGplayer listing ($49.15).
- Odds (data/pullrates.json): burpies' 1,580 packs (Elite Fourum) for Shiny 21.2%, Shiny GX 10.19%, GX 15.06%, Rare Ultra 4.87% (split 3.73% main / 1.14% vault by ThePriceDex's ratio), Rainbow 1.71%, vault gold 1.77%. Holo Rare 20% is ThePriceDex only (single source). The vault sits in the reverse slot (34.3% of packs). Reverse-holo Energy (ThePriceDex 1 in 20) is not modelled.
- Foil maps: TCG Live `sm11-5`, main 1-69 as numbered, Shiny Vault SV n = 69 + n (`GALLERY_KEY`), checked by overlaying all 94 vault maps and all 19 main-set maps on their scans. 119 cards have a foil map (19 main holos, all 94 vault cards, 6 promos); the 94 vault cards and the 4 full arts also have an etch map at 400 px. `make_etch_hd.py` was NOT run: `sm-shiny.css` owners decide whether the vault wants the native 733 px etch (`etchHd`) as Shining Fates does. No map: the commons, uncommons, plain Rares.
- Promo finishes (`smbsp` maps, numbers match): smp210 (ETB full art, stained-glass etch), smp211-213 GX, smp214/215 Mewtwo and Mew. smp103a and smp104a (the gold Lunala-GX and Solgaleo-GX of the Premium Powers and Ultra-Premium collections) have no map: TCG Live files only the plain SM103/SM104.
- Products (TCGplayer market prices; 2+ sources each in the research): Mew and Mewtwo pin collections, three GX tins, three GX collections, Elite Trainer Box (10 packs, id `elite-trainer-box`), Premium Powers and Ultra-Premium collections. Not built: the two Poke Ball Collections (their Shiny Zoroark-GX 77a and Shiny Metagross-GX 157a alt arts are cards of sm35 and sm2) and the Rayquaza 177a of the two premium boxes (sm7); add them with `like: "sm35:77a"` when those sets carry the prints.
- Oddity: `smp103a`/`smp104a` need the letter-suffix support added to `build_products.py` (pkmn.gg page `SM103a`, scrydex `smp-SM103a`); `promoSeries.smp.scrydexKey` is `SM{n:02d}` because pkmn.gg's page key is `SM001` but scrydex files `smp-SM01`.
