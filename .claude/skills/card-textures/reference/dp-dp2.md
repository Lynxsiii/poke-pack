# Diamond & Pearl: Mysterious Treasures (dp2)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder d1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/dp/research/dp2.md`, `dp2-products.md`, `dpp-claims.md`, `theme-decks.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Release 2007-08-22. 124 cards (123 printed + the secret Trainer Time-Space Distortion 124; C 42, U 37, R 23, RH 18, LV.X 3), plus two theme-deck holo prints (`dp2-21-theme` Bastiodon, `dp2-33-theme` Rampardos, counted in the set: totalCount 126) and three promos filed as `dp2-dppNN`.

## Pack
Ten cards, no basic Energy (the Special Energy are ordinary Uncommons and a Rare). Rare .6667, Rare Holo .3005, LV.X .0278, secret .0051 (Elite Fourum's sheet model puts Time-Space Distortion twice on the holo sheet; Flipside reports 1 in 72 and Elite Fourum's own 10-box sample saw one in 360 packs, so the figure is LOW confidence and sits between them). data/pullrates.json.

## Scans and wrappers
Scrydex `dp2-<n>`; looked at Aggron (first), Tyranitar, Toxicroak, the three LV.X, Time-Space Distortion (last) and Magmortar: clean. Wrappers `mysterious-treasures-pack-0..3` cut in place: Toxicroak, Uxie/Mesprit/Azelf, Magmortar, Garchomp.

## Maps
None (TCG Live has no DP maps).

## Products
Five built: Theme Decks "Skull Charge" ($76.99) and "Armor Fortress" ($74.96), TCGplayer 229176/229177, each with a deck-exclusive cosmos holofoil print of a card that is a plain Rare in packs (Rampardos, Bastiodon; one source, Collexy) as the deck's promo card; the Three Pack Blisters [Cranidos] DP07 and [Shieldon] DP08 (2 dp2 packs, no TCGplayer listing, derived price); the Lucario Collector's Tin DP12 (3 dp3 + 1 dp2 packs, medium-high; filed here by the promo claim table; derived at 4 x the dp2 pack).
Left out: booster box (no price), Buneary blister DP06 (one source, and retailers call it a 2-pack), Value Pack 1 (pack count unread), prerelease kit (not documented; the Gabite stamp promo has no product), Poster Box (one source, promo not numbered).
