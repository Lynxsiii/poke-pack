# Wizards of the Coast: Neo Discovery (neo2)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder w3). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/wotc/research/neo2.md`, `neo2-products.md`, `wotc-pack-structure.md`, `theme-decks.md`.
Release 2001-06-01. English Unlimited prints only. 75 cards: Rare Holo 17, Rare 19, Uncommon 19, Common 20. No basic Energy (Energy Ark 75 is a Trainer); the research's retailer claim of two Energy a pack is wrong.

## Scans
All 75 checked on contact sheets: aligned, no borders. 1st Edition stamp on every scan (same on every source); none swapped. Unown cards come from pkmn.gg named `Unown [A] (14)`: fetch_tcgplayer_prices.py norm() strips that to Unown.

## Pack
11 cards: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons, a rare slot; no Energy, no reverse. `wotcSlots({ holo: 0.3333 })`. Holo 1 in 3 (WotC chat, PSA 9436, Flipside, EF): high.

## Wrapper
Four, `neo-discovery-pack-N`: Smeargle, Xatu, Umbreon, Scizor (cards neo2-11, 52, 13, 10).

## Prices
TCGplayer Unlimited rows, 74 of 75 priced: **neo2-26 Magnemite has no TCGplayer row** and stays unpriced (never invented). Top: Umbreon 13 $752.74, Espeon 1 $365.17. **Pack $537.50.** Products: 2-pack Blister (282509), and theme deck Brain Wave (234510, price from the product page marketPrice $425, shop-visible through its promo neo2-14).
