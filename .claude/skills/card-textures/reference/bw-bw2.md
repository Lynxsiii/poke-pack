# Black & White: Emerging Powers (bw2)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/bw/research/bw2.md`, `bw2-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`, `theme-decks.md`.
Released 2011-08-31. 98 cards (no secret rare) plus 5 reprints = `totalCount` 103; 10 `bw2-bwpNNN` promos home to `bwp`. TCGplayer group 1424, wrapper art `emerging-powers`.

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy; from this set every pack carries a code card (not one of the 10). `bwSlots({ holo: .2778, ultra: .0556 })`: EF "same box ratios as Black & White, except the set has no secret rares" (60 / 12 of 216), checked against PokeBeach (holo-or-better 1 in 3, "two, three if you're lucky" full arts per box) and Flipside's era figure. Only EF gives per-set numbers: medium. Reverse pool C/U/R/Rare Holo; the reverse is the new Energy-symbol pattern (Bulbapedia Emerging Powers page: first set with it).

## Scans and maps
scrydex, 98 of 98; checked 1, 77 (holo), 97, 98 (full arts). TCG Live key `bw2`: 12 of 12 foil prints (10 Rare Holo, Thundurus 97, Tornadus 98) and the 10 promo maps (bwbsp).
