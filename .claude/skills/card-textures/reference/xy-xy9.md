# XY: BREAKpoint (xy9)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder b4). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/xy/research/xy9.md`, `xy9-products.md`, `xyp-claims.md`.
126 cards on pkmn.gg; the file holds 123 (122 printed + the shiny Gyarados-EX secret 123) plus the promo cards `xy9-xyp095/096/106/107/108/109` (counted under xyp). Rarities: 33 Common, 41 Uncommon, 16 Rare, 7 Rare Holo, 11 Rare Holo EX (2 Mega), 5 Rare BREAK, 9 Rare Ultra, 1 Rare Secret.
`98a`, `98b` (Delinquent) and `107a` (Professor Sycamore) are prize prints no product holds: `skipNumbers`, scans not shipped.

## Pack
10 cards: 5 Commons, 3 Uncommons, reverse, rare slot, no Energy. BREAK takes the reverse slot (E2, PokeBeach report 1025, Dripshop). Rates (E2 sheet model on 28 boxes; Danny Phantump, Derium, TCAGaming second counts; high): holo .1667, EX .0818, Mega EX .0182 (card share, low), full art .0587, secret .008 (low), BREAK .0667.

## Scans
Scrydex `xy9-<n>`. Checked 1, 3, 14, 98b, 18, 123 by eye, plus a pixel comparison of every scan against pkmn.gg's image (only one differed). `xy9-3` Meganium: scrydex AND pokemontcg.io both show the League-stamped print; the unstamped pack card (pkmn.gg `xy9-003v2`, TCGplayer 111506 agrees) replaces it (re-encoded to 600x838 and 320x447).

## Wrappers
pokesymbols `breakpoint-pack-0..3` (opaque, cut in place with clean_pack_art.py): Greninja BREAK, Luxray BREAK, Shiny Mega Gyarados, Mega Scizor (Bulbapedia + viewed; viewed on a dark ground, clean).

## Maps
TCG Live key `xy9`: all 33 foil prints (7 holo, 11 EX, 5 BREAK, 9 full art, 1 secret) have a foil map, 10 etch maps. Overlaid on the scans, aligned.

## Products
ETB (8 packs, $1,099.99; filed as product `elite-trainer-box`, not in sealed.json), Pikachu and Hydreigon single blisters (Hydreigon's promo is a Phantom Forces galaxy reprint, not built), Umbreon and Pyroar 3-pack blisters (Pyroar likewise), two collector-pin blisters, Shiny Mega Gyarados Collection (real mix 2 xy9 + xy8 + xy7), three Triple Power Tins. Promo `foilAs`: Rare Holo EX for the EX tins, Rare Holo for the Cosmos ones. Where TCGplayer has no price the product is priced from its packs at the booster pack price (`priceDerived: "packs"`).
