# Wizards of the Coast: Neo Revelation (neo3)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder w3). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/wotc/research/neo3.md`, `neo3-products.md`, `wotc-pack-structure.md`.
Release 2001-09-21. English Unlimited prints only. 66 cards (printed 64): Rare Holo 14, Rare 13, Uncommon 17, Common 20, **Rare Shining 2** (65 Shining Gyarados, 66 Shining Magikarp). No Energy, no theme decks (Wizards said earlier theme decks sold poorly).

## Scans
All 66 checked: aligned, no borders, 1st Edition stamp throughout (no other source); none swapped. The two Shinings scan cleanly.

## Pack
11 cards: 7 Commons, 3 Uncommons, a rare slot; no reverse. `wotcSlots({ holo: 0.2972, rareSpecial: [["shining", 0.0364]] })`; the shining pool is the two Rare Shining cards.
**Shining rate: the sources disagree and the research gives a range.** PSA 9458 "probably one per box" = 0.028 a pack; Flipside "about 1:18" = 0.056; Elite Fourum box tallies "0 to 2 a box". Elite Fourum's sheet model (the two Shinings assumed at x6 on the 110-position holo sheet, 2/55) = 0.0364 sits between the two ends and is used (their plain midpoint is 0.042). Medium-low. Holo 0.2972 = 14 holos at 1 in 47.1.

## Finish
Regular holos `wotc-cosmos`. Shining: "glossy metallic look" on the Pokémon only, background not holo (Bulbapedia, PSA 10154); `foil-holo wotc-shining` (js/wotc-foil.js).

## Wrapper
Four, `neo-revelation-pack-N`: Misdreavus, Raikou, Suicune, Entei (neo3-11, 13, 14, 6); PSA 9458 names the same four.

## Prices
All 66 priced: Shining Gyarados $782.55, Shining Magikarp $445.00, Houndoom $347.50. **Pack $945.00** (mid of the live listings: TCGplayer has no market price). Booster box built at 36 packs with no TCGplayer id (derived from the pack price). 1st Edition products not built.
