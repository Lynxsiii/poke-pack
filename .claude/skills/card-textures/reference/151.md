# Scarlet & Violet 151 (sv3pt5)

What the 151 thread learned taking the set to its real finishes
(2026-09-27). Every pack print is a finish the site already draws or one of
the shared Scarlet & Violet recipes; what is new here is the Cosmos holo
basic Energies, the retailer cosmos promos, the Costco "reverse cosmos"
promos, and two tools: an upstream reference page and an app-exact renderer.

## The set in TCG Live's export

Key `sv3-5` (not `sv3pt5`), 360 records: 207 `_std_` prints plus 153 `_ph_`
reverse prints (Commons, Uncommons and Rares only, all `FLAT_SILVER` /
`REVERSE`). English 151 has no Poké Ball or Master Ball reverses (those are
Japanese 151 and, in English, Prismatic Evolutions), no Tera ex and no ACE
SPEC.

## Cosmos holo basic Energies

About one pack in four (TCGplayer: 24.83%) has a galaxy holo basic Energy
in the Energy slot. They are not in the 207-card list. TCG Live files them
under key `sve`, numbers 001-008, as the `_ph_` print:
`BasicGEnergy_sve_1_ph_CommonBasicEnergy_Cosmos_Reverse`, `COSMOS` /
`REVERSE`. All eight maps are the same picture (the whole card body foil
except the Energy symbol and its swirl, the name bar's lettering foil), so
the site ships one, `assets/foil/maps/sv3pt5/cosmos-energy.webp`.

In the data they are cards of the set: `sv3pt5-cosmos-<type>`, number
`"—"`, `energy: true`, `foilAs: "Cosmos"`, the shared basic Energy scans,
TCGplayer prices (group 24382). `foilLook()` takes `energy` cards as well as
promos, so they draw as `foil-holo foil-cosmos foil-energy has-foil-map`.
`poolsFrom()` splits Energies into `energy` (no `foilAs`) and `foilEnergy`
(with one), and the set's Energy slot rolls between them:
`{ pool: "energy", count: 1, table: [["energy", 0.7517], ["foilEnergy", 0.2483]] }`.

`fetch_foil_maps.py --write-data` used to strip `foilMap` from any card whose
number is not in the export (`"—"`, `"63-cosmos"`); it now leaves those
cards' maps alone, so hand-made maps survive a re-run.

## Retailer cosmos promos: use the clean scan

TCGplayer's photo of a cosmos holo print (Sam's Club tin Raichu, Machamp,
Gengar, Dragonite; ex Collection Abra, Kadabra, Electabuzz; Costco's
Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle) bakes the foil in: a scanner sees
foil as dark, so the silver border comes out black with stars and the art
comes out darker and dotted. Drawing the site's galaxy holo over that
doubles the foil and reads as a dirty card. `build_products.py` now takes
`"keepScan": true` on a `like` promo: the promo keeps the set card's clean
scan and the foil comes from the recipe alone.

What the photos do show is where the foil is: the dots cover the silver
border and the art background, never the Pokémon (Abra's and Electabuzz's
bodies are clean). That is exactly a holo Rare's TCG Live map, so the
cosmos prints of Rares keep the Rare's map (`"keepMaps": true`). Commons
and Uncommons have no map; `"foilMap"` in the promo spec now names a
hand-made one. Abra, Kadabra and Electabuzz got the border ring of a Rare
map with the same layout (113 Chansey for a Basic, 26 Raichu for a Stage 1)
plus the whole art window (`--win-holo`'s rectangle, or `win-evo.png` for
the Stage 1), so the Pokémon is foil too. A difference mask between the
clean scan and the cosmos photo, to cut the Pokémon out, was too noisy
(misregistration, JPEG) to use.

The metal Mew ex from the Ultra-Premium Collection keeps its own photo: a
brushed-metal card really does look different from the paper gold 205.

## Reverse cosmos (Costco)

The four Costco mini tin promos are reverse holos with the galaxy holo on
the frame instead of flat silver. `foilAs: "Reverse Cosmos"` makes
`foilLook()` return a reverse with `reverseKind: "galaxy"`, and
`foilPattern()` draws it as `foil-reverse foil-cosmos`: windows.css's
reverse mask with promos.css's three galaxy layers inside it. No new CSS.
(`reverseKind: "cosmos"` already existed and means the standard reverse,
hence the different name.)

## Demigod packs

English 151 has "demigod packs": one starter's line in illustration art,
Basic IR + Stage 1 IR + ex SIR (166-167-198, 168-169-199, 170-171-200).
PokeBeach's set guide (2023-09-21) describes them; Dexerto reported a real
Charizard one (2024-02-23). Nobody has measured the rate; the set uses 1 in
1,300, a community guess. `set.godPack = { rate, lines }` in
js/catalog.js, and `rollGodPack()` in js/engine.js puts the three cards
into the `reverse`, `rare-slot` and `art-slot` slots in that order.

## Tools

- **`lab/upstream151.html`** draws our cards with pokemon-cards-151's own
  CSS (`upstream/sv151/`) and TCG Live's raw maps from `lab/cache/<set>/`,
  at the lab's poses: `?set=sv3pt5&cards=183,196,205&poses=30:25,65:70`.
  Use it to compare a site recipe with the upstream it came from. Two
  traps it works around: upstream's CSS asks for `/img/grain.webp` and
  `/img/glitter.webp` (ours stand in), and Chrome resolves a relative
  `url()` inside a custom property against the stylesheet that uses the
  `var()`, not the one that sets it, so every texture and map path it sets
  is absolute. Headless Chromium behind a proxy with its own certificate
  authority cannot reach upstream's CDN; download the textures with curl
  into `lab/cache/upstream151/` and add `&tex=cache` (the page's comment
  has the loop; `iri-8.webp` can reset the connection, retry it).
- **`scripts/site_cards.mjs`** renders card ids exactly as the app does,
  `foilLook()`, reverse kinds and all, by clicking the app's own inspector,
  then pins each row to a pose: `--set sv3pt5 --ids sv3pt5-25-costco,...
  --rev sv3pt5-25 --poses rest,30:25`. It prints each card's classes.
  Promos, retailer prints and Energies with a `foilAs` only render right
  here, not in the lab.
