# Sun & Moon: Crimson Invasion (sm4)

Built 2026-09-30 (builder b2). Research: `/mnt/project-files/sun-moon/research/sm4.md` plus `second-source-check.md` section 6.
126 cards on pkmn.gg (111 printed + 13 secrets + 63a + 84a), all in data/sm4.json, plus 14 promo cards (140 in all; 63a is one of the 126 and also a product promo).

## Pack
Eleven cards: basic Energy (`sm17`), 5 Commons, 3 Uncommons, a reverse holo, a rare slot (plain Rare in 2 of 3 packs). No Prism Star, no special slot.
Rates: holo .1842 (1/3 minus the hits), GX .0835, full art .0443, rainbow .0104, gold .0109. The pooled tallies (E2 own n=1,981, burpies n=1,530,
Derium n=1,008) agree within a point. The rainbow/gold split is the raw per-card count (own + Derium: rainbow 30, gold 32; burpies 50/50), so gold
items and Energy are pulled as often as the rainbows (49% rainbow), not the card-count rule's 61.5% (second-source check). Plain Rare = 2/3
of packs is counted directly (E2 post 145, 1,317 of 1,981 packs). Sources and links are in data/pullrates.json.

## `a` prints
`63a` Guzzlord-GX (alternate art) is the promo of the Guzzlord GX Box (TCGplayer 155061): built as promo `sm4-63a` (`like` itself, `keepScan`,
`foilAs` Rare Holo GX). `84a` Regigigas (cosmos holo, in sm6's 3-pack blisters) stays as a set entry with `promo: true` for sm6's `like: "sm4:84a"`
(rarity and `foilAs` Rare Holo); build_products.py keeps a `promo` card whose rarity is not "Promo". Prices are the base card's.

## Maps
TCG Live key `sm4`. 58 of the 60 foil prints have a foil map (every holo, GX, full art, rainbow and gold and every promo), 24 etch maps. No map: 63a and 84a
(TCG Live has no `a` prints). Overlaid on the scans: all line up.

single-pack blisters (Golisopod SM52, Dhelmise SM53), the two Ultra Beasts GX Premium Collections (8 packs; SM66 Pheromosa-GX + SM67 Celesteela-GX;
SM68 Xurkitree-GX + SM69 Buzzwole-GX), Shiny Silvally GX Box (SM91), Marshadow Figure Collection (SM85), Guzzlord GX Box (63a), the Pikachu and Mimikyu
Sidekick Collections (SM98, SM99; 3 packs). The Shiny Silvally, figure and sidekick boxes have no documented pack mix: shown as Crimson Invasion packs. No TCGplayer market price, so left out:
the Lucario and Decidueye 3-pack blisters (SM54, SM55) and the Silvally Figure Collection (SM64). Finishes: holo for the Pokémon, GX for the GX. Booster Box $678 and pack $18.22 (TCGplayer).
