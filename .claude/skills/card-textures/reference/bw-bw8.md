# Black & White: Plasma Storm (bw8)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c3). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/bw/research/bw8.md`, `bw8-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `box-contents.md`, `second-source-check.md`.
Release 2013-02-06. 138 cards (135 printed + Charizard, Blastoise and the secret Item Random Receiver): C 41, U 42, Rare 26, Rare Holo 10, Rare Holo EX 8, Rare Ultra 5, Rare ACE 3, Rare Secret 3. Twelve promo cards (counted under bwp). First set with blue-bordered Team Plasma cards (pkmn.gg has them as ordinary cards).

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy; Ace Spec in the reverse slot: `bwSlots({ holo: .2222, ex: .0741, ultra: .0237, secret: .0133, aceSpec: .0417 })`. Sources as bw7 (EF #116, 28 boxes; PokeBeach pages 19-22). EX and Ace Spec medium; the full-art / secret split (secrets 36% of an 8-in-216 shared sheet) is low-medium: EF itself flags "small irregularities" and PokeBeach calls the set's rates "all over the place".
The Team Plasma sub-sheets (exactly 1 Team Plasma uncommon + 2 regular in every pack; rare, holo and reverse sheets split regular / Team Plasma) are not modelled; the combined rates are used (shares are in the research file).
Reverse pool: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Rare Holo (TCGplayer reverse rows).

## Scans and wrappers
Scrydex for all 138. Looked at 1, 17, 84, 108, 128, 134, 136, 138 and the promos: clean. Wrappers: pokesymbols `plasma-storm-pack-0..3`: Liepard, Infernape, Lugia, Articuno (checked against Bulbapedia's wrapper table), cut with clean_pack_art.py.

## Maps
TCG Live key `bw8`: 29 of 29 foil prints (RH 10, EX 8, RU 5, RS 3, ACE 3), 8 with an etch map (the full arts and secrets); 11 promo maps from `bwbsp` (BW58, 59, 61-64, 66, 67, 70-72; Jigglypuff BW65 is non-holo). Overlaid: Infernape, Lugia-EX (both), Dowsing Machine, Charizard, Random Receiver: fine.

## Prices
tcgcsv group 1413: 138 of 138 singles; booster pack $549.96 (tcgcsv; low $999.99). No box price, no ETB price.

## Products (11)
The first Elite Trainer Box (seven packs, 40 basic Energy, 2 acrylic markers, 6 dice, a coin-flip die, a guide and a deck box, no promo, no sleeves: Bulbapedia + ToyWiz + rarecards.nl; `elite-trainer-box`, kind etb with its real 7 packs; the box-contents row `bw8/elite-trainer-box` deals 5 Energy of each type in the `bw` print), the Ninetales and Ampharos 3 Pack Blisters (BW66, BW67), the Drifblim-line and Wigglytuff-line Stage 1 Blisters (BW64 Drifblim, BW65 Jigglypuff), the Legends of Justice Box (BW70-72, 3 packs), three EX Power Tins (BW61, BW62, BW63; four packs), the Black Kyurem Box (206306, BW58) and the White Kyurem Box (206307, BW59). Pack mixes are Bulbapedia only ("typically ..."): where Bulbapedia names only some of the packs, the rest are dealt as Plasma Storm packs and the product's `note` says so. Prices are derived from the packs at the Plasma Storm pack price plus the promos (nothing is on TCGplayer with a market price).
