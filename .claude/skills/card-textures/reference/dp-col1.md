# Call of Legends (col1)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder d4). Research: /mnt/project-files/every-era/hgss/research/col1.md, hgss-pack-structure.md, hgss-products.md, hsp-claims.md, second-source-check.md.
Released 2011-02-09. 106 cards (95 printed + SL1-SL11) plus 2 `col1-hspNN` promos. Scrydex `col1`, pkmn.gg `call-of-legends`, TCGplayer 1415. 22 Rare Holo, 17 Rare, 25 Uncommon, 23 Common, 8 basic Energy (88-95, `reverse: true`, rarity "Energy"), 11 SL cards (`rarityOverrides` "Rare Shiny Legendary"). No LEGEND, Prime or secret.

## Pack
`dpSlots({ holo: 0.3333, reverseSpecial: [["shinyLegend", 0.0556], ["reverseEnergy", 0.0370]] })`: a holo exactly every third pack, one Shiny Legendary in 18 packs (two a box), the eight Energy reverse-only (8 of 216 packs' reverse slot). Elite Fourum + Flipside (the Shiny Legendary rate agrees in both: high).

## Scans / wrappers
Scrydex, 8 looked at (including SL1, SL11, two Energy), clean. Wrappers `call-of-legends-pack-0..3` (Deoxys, Groudon, Lugia, Kyogre), opaque tight photos, kept.
