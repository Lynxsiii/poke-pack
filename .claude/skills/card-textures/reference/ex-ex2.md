# EX era: EX Sandstorm (ex2)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder e1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/ex/research/ex2.md`, `ex2-products.md`, `ex-pack-structure.md`, `ex-finish-types.md`, `second-source-check.md`. Release 2003-09-17 (Serebii; TCGplayer's group date is the 18th). 100 cards (100 printed).

## Cards
Rare Holo 14 (1-14: Armaldo, Cacturne, Cradily, Dusclops, Flareon, Jolteon, Ludicolo, Lunatone, Mawile, Sableye, Seviper, Shiftry, Solrock, Zangoose), Rare Holo EX 7 (94-100: Aerodactyl ex, Aggron ex, Gardevoir ex, Kabutops ex, Raichu ex, Typhlosion ex, Wailord ex), Rare 13 (93 is Multi Energy), Uncommon 33, Common 33. No basic Energy, no secret, no Gold Star. Counts match pkmn.gg. Scans: scrydex `ex2-<n>`; looked at 1, 4, 50, 70, 88, 93, 96, 100: straight, none swapped; the automatic edge check of all 100 flagged none.

## Pack
Nine cards, early layout as Ruby & Sapphire: 4 Commons, the fifth slot (holo 1/6, Pokemon-ex 1/6, else a common), 2 Uncommons, a Mirror reverse (93 cards), an always-plain Rare. `exSlots({early: true, holo: .1667, ex: .1667})`. Elite Fourum post 36 (252-pack tally, "basically the same as Ruby & Sapphire") with Flipside Gaming's 1:12-18: medium. `reverseTwice` empty (EF's table image lists 17 doubled reverse cards without names in the post).

## For the finish agents
Cosmos holo on the 14 holos and 7 ex. The dot strip IS present on holo, ex, reverse and non-holo cards (every scan shows the 13 lines at the foot). Mirror reverse (body foiled, art box plain), same as ex1. No refractor, cracked ice, delta, topper.
