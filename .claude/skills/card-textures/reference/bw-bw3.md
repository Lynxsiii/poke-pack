# Black & White: Noble Victories (bw3)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder c1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/bw/research/bw3.md`, `bw3-products.md`, `bwp-claims.md`, `second-source-check.md`, `theme-decks.md`.
Released 2011-11-16. 101 printed, 102 with the secret Meowth, plus 2 reprints = `totalCount` 104; 4 `bw3-bwpNNN` promos home to `bwp`. TCGplayer group 1385, wrapper art `noble-victories`.

## Pack
Ten cards, no Energy, code card. `bwSlots({ holo: .2639, ultra: .0556, secret: .0139 })`: EF "same box ratios as Black & White" (57 / 12 / 3 of 216), Flipside (ultra rares ~1:18, secrets ~1:72), PokeBeach ("two, three if you're lucky" full arts a box). Full arts (five: Virizion, Victini, Terrakion, Cobalion, N, the first full-art Trainer) high confidence; the Meowth secret medium (PokeBeach's 2011 "108 to 1" dissents). Reverse: Energy-symbol pattern.
