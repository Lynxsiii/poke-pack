# HS Triumphant (hgss4)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder d4). Research: /mnt/project-files/every-era/hgss/research/hgss4.md, hgss-pack-structure.md, hgss-products.md, hsp-claims.md, second-source-check.md.
Released 2010-11-03. 103 cards (102 printed + Alph Lithograph FOUR) plus 2 `hgss4-hspNN` promos. Scrydex `hgss4`, pkmn.gg `hs-triumphant`, TCGplayer 1381. 12 Rare Holo, 19 Rare, 29 Uncommon, 30 Common, 8 Rare Prime (91-98), 4 LEGEND (99-102: Darkrai & Cresselia, Palkia & Dialga), 1 Rare Secret (FOUR). Rescue Energy (90) is an Uncommon; no basic Energy. Nidoran F/M (69/70) are `Nidoran` in the price matcher.
