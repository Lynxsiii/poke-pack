# XY: XY (xy1)

Built 2026-10-01 (builder b1). Research: `/mnt/project-files/every-era/xy/research/xy1.md`, `xy1-products.md`, `xyp-claims.md`.
146 cards on pkmn.gg and in the file (+2 promo cards `xy1-xyp007/008`, counted under xyp). Rarities: 39 Common, 42 Uncommon, 26 Rare, 16 Rare Holo, 8 Rare Holo EX (2 Mega), 6 Rare Ultra, 9 basic Energy (132-140, filed "Energy").

## Pack
10 cards: 5 Commons, 3 Uncommons, 1 reverse, 1 rare slot, no Energy slot. Rates (E2 sheet model, n = 29 boxes; PokeBeach box thread as second count; medium): holo .2333, EX .0625, Mega EX .0208, full art .0278, no secret rares. Mega share of the EX sheet is card share (low). The nine basic Energy sit on the commons sheet: `commonEnergy: .455` (E2 model only, ThePriceDex copies it: low).

## Scans
Scrydex `xy1-<n>`. Checked 1, 8, 100, 135, 141, 142, 144, 146: clean, no white edges.

## Wrappers
pokesymbols `x-and-y-pack-0..3` (opaque JPG-like renders; cut with clean_pack_art.py, cleaned in place because untracked): Yveltal, Xerneas, Mega Blastoise, Mega Venusaur (Bulbapedia order table + pokesymbols alt text "Blastoise"/"Venusaur" + viewed).

## Maps
TCG Live key `xy1`: all 16 Rare Holo, 8 EX, 6 full arts (with etch maps) and both promos have a foil map; overlaid on the scans, aligned. Plain Rare/Common/Uncommon/Energy have none (not foil).

## Products
Only the TCGplayer-priced ones are built: Legend of Kalos Tins [Xerneas EX] (XY07) and [Yveltal EX] (XY08). TCGplayer has no market price for the two ETBs, the Kalos Starter Figure Boxes (XY01-03), Sylveon Collection (XY04), the Xerneas/Yveltal Figure Collections (XY05, XY06), Garchomp EX Box (XY09) and the two pin blisters, so build_products leaves them out; they stay in data/sets/xy1.json and appear once TCGplayer prices them. Packs of every box are shown as XY packs (the Kalos starter boxes and Sylveon Collection held Black & White packs, the tins 2 XY + Legendary Treasures + Plasma Blast). Promo `foilAs`: Rare Holo EX for the EX/Mirror promos, Rare Holo for the Cosmos ones. Booster box $3,900, pack $133.34.
